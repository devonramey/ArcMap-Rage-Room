WELCOME TO THE IMPACT CHAMBER

An offline cartoon physics sandbox starring an ArcMap splash-screen mascot. Throw the target against empty chamber walls. The game never accesses GIS software or files.

ATTACKSelect SWORD and drag across the mascot to slice. Select FLAMETHROWER to hold down fire and char the surface. Try FREEZE RAY, TESLA COIL, 50 CAL, or CANNON.
GRAB & THROWSelect GRAB to move the mascot directly with your mouse. Right-drag works with any weapon. Throw it into the walls and floor.
DROP OBJECTSUse rockets and grenades for explosive knockback. Bullet holes and scorch marks persist until Reset.
FINISHERPress F (or click the finisher button) to equip the Cricket Gun. Hold the left mouse button until its charge reaches 100%, aim, then release to fire. Only a direct hit blows ArcMap apart. The pieces persist until Clean / Repair; a miss does nothing.
WEAPON INTERACTIONSHeat + freeze + railgun can cause shattering. Water enhances electricity and puts out flames. Nails, webs and harpoons add real tension restraints. Stasis stores momentum and releases it when the field ends.
EXPERIMENTAL TOOLSPlace linked portals, spawn a gravity well or loading vortex, grow and shrink the mascot, glassify it, spawn clones, dissolve it into pixels, or transform it into a cookie.
MORE CONTROLSQ/E weapons, Space clean and repair, B red effects, G gravity, P pause, M mute, V character voice, H help, L limbs. Use the loadout category filters to find experimental weapons.
